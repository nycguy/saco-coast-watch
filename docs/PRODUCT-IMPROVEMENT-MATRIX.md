## Source coverage finding

The “2 of 5” value was emitted by the standalone NWS/NHC hazard builder. That builder does not collect NOAA water levels/tides or NDBC buoy conditions, but the former schema counted all unrequested inputs as missing. This is a SacoCast coverage-accounting defect, not evidence that NOAA or NDBC was unavailable. The integrated briefing is the source of truth for the combined coastal rating: its production snapshot had four of five inputs available. The fifth, current NDBC wave height, was absent from the latest 44007 observation even though a 24-hour maximum existed; the application should retain that distinction and should not treat the older maximum as a current reading.
