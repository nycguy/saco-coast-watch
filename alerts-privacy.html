Coastal Storm Alerts: Privacy

Last updated September 2026. This policy applies to the optional email and SMS alerts, not official NOAA or NWS systems.

Information collected

On a confirmed subscription, the independent alert service stores the email address, optional mobile phone number, selected thresholds, channel consents and verification status, notification delivery state, and timestamps. A short-lived, one-way identifier may be used to limit signup abuse. The main GitHub Pages dashboard does not store the subscriber database.

Use and disclosure

Information is used solely to verify subscriptions, check public weather/water data, deliver requested alerts, handle changes or cancellation, and prevent abuse. Cloudflare operates the backend/database, Resend processes requested email, and Twilio and telecommunications carriers process opted-in SMS. Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell subscriber information.

Control and retention

Subscribers can use the management link requested from the dashboard, or the unsubscribe link in any delivered alert, to stop both email and SMS. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from texts. Confirmation and management links expire. The operator should periodically delete inactive subscriptions and expired records from its secured database, subject to necessary consent or compliance records.

Contact

To request access or deletion beyond unsubscribing, use the support address supplied in the subscription confirmation email. This service will not accept registrations until the operator has supplied a working support contact and verified its privacy and consent setup.
