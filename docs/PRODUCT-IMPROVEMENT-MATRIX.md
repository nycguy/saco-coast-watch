## Source coverage finding

The “2 of 5” value was emitted by the standalone NWS/NHC hazard builder. That builder does not collect NOAA water levels/tides or NDBC buoy conditions, but the former schema counted all unrequested inputs as missing. That was a SacoCast coverage-accounting defect, not evidence that those providers were down. The integrated briefing had four of five inputs at 16:17 UTC, five of five at 17:59 UTC, and four of five at 18:13 UTC on October 14. The production snapshot identifies the NWS Surf Zone product as stale (latest issuance October 12) and shows the other inputs as current; this is an upstream update delay, not a parsing failure. Stale surf values are not used for current hazard modes or impact scoring. A live NDBC response showed a newer wind row with `WVHT=MM` and a 17:20 UTC row with a valid 1.4 m wave measurement. Taking only the newest row for every metric hid this still-fresh wave value. The collector now selects and timestamps wind and wave samples independently. The original 2/5 check is explained by producer scope; the subsequent stale-surf and missing-wave symptoms had separate causes.
