Coastal Storm Alerts: Terms and SMS consent

Last updated September 2026. Alerts are optional and informational, not an official emergency warning service.

Subscription and delivery

After confirming email and, separately, any requested SMS, users may receive alerts when their selected observed or modeled conditions reach a configured threshold. Message frequency varies with conditions. A first alert may occur when an already-active condition is first checked after confirmation. Notifications are best effort, usually evaluated on a five-minute schedule, and may be delayed or unavailable due to outages or source updates. The operator does not guarantee delivery or accuracy.

Text messaging

By checking the separate, initially unchecked SMS consent box, providing a mobile number, confirming the email request, and confirming the separate SMS verification message, you agree to receive automated, non-marketing threshold alerts from Saco Coast Watch. Consent is not required for any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts; reply HELP for assistance if supported by the registered sender. Each alert also contains an unsubscribe link. Number of messages varies based on selected thresholds. Ask the operator for help using the support email provided in the subscription confirmation.

Weather and water information

Portland tide-gauge levels do not determine flood depth at any individual property in Saco Bay. NOAA model water levels are forecasts, not verified observations; measured winds are from offshore buoy 44007; temperature is measured at Portland Jetport, not the beach. Severe waves, shoreline changes, and local conditions can produce impacts not shown here. For protective action, consult NWS Gray and local emergency officials.
