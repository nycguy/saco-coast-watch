SacoCast Notification Privacy

Updated November 28, 2026. SacoCast offers optional email alerts and app notifications. These are independent of official NOAA and NWS warning systems.

Email Alerts

The alert service stores your verified email address, selected thresholds, channel consent, confirmation and management tokens, delivery state, and timestamps in Cloudflare D1. Resend processes requested emails. Short-lived hashed identifiers help limit signup abuse. The current public signup does not collect phone numbers or offer SMS.

App Notifications

App notifications require your action and browser permission. The service stores a random device identifier, a hash of its control credential, push endpoint and encryption keys, preferences, and delivery state. Your browser stores the device identifier and control credential. Email is optional and is linked only through address verification.

Notifications can describe coastal-event changes or selected custom thresholds. The push provider receives the encrypted notification and delivery endpoint. Notification history includes recent event notices and the latest successful custom-threshold delivery for each metric and channel; it is not a complete attempt log.

Your Controls

Turn off app notifications in SacoCast or your browser settings. Use a verified management link or an unsubscribe link to manage email alerts. Turning off one delivery channel does not automatically turn off an independent channel. Unsubscribing stops delivery but does not immediately erase every stored record.

Use and Contact

Subscription information is used for verification, requested notifications, account controls, delivery reliability, and abuse prevention. We do not sell subscription information. For support or a deletion request, use the support address in your subscription email or verified alert-management page.
