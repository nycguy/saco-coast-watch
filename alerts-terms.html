SacoCast Notification Terms

Updated November 28, 2026. Optional SacoCast notifications are informational and best effort. They are not official emergency warnings.

Channels and Preferences

Email alerts require address verification. App notifications require a supported device, browser permission, and separate enrollment. App notifications can cover coastal-event starts, ends, meaningful changes, and selected custom thresholds. Email delivery uses your verified threshold preferences. SMS is not offered by the current public signup.

Timing and Availability

Delivery depends on data sources, scheduled checks, connectivity, browser services, and providers. Messages may arrive late or fail. A selected condition may already be active when first checked after enrollment. A source check does not guarantee a new observation or forecast. Do not rely on SacoCast notifications for protective action.

Weather and Water Information

Portland gauge heights are regional reference measurements, not flood depth at a Saco property. Astronomical tides exclude weather effects; NOAA model guidance is modeled rather than observed water. Offshore buoy waves and winds are not local breaking surf or property measurements. Waves, erosion, runup, and flooding can occur locally even below Portland thresholds.

Managing Notifications

Manage app notifications from the device where you enrolled. Manage email through a verified link or unsubscribe from a delivered email. A test notification is sent only when explicitly requested for an enrolled device. For help, use the support contact in your verified alert-management page or email.

For official guidance, consult NWS Gray / Portland and local emergency officials.
