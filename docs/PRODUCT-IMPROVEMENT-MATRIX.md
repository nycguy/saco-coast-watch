## Source coverage finding

The “2 of 5” value was emitted by the standalone NWS/NHC hazard builder. That builder does not collect NOAA water levels/tides or NDBC buoy conditions, but the former schema counted all unrequested inputs as missing. This is a SacoCast coverage-accounting defect, not evidence that NOAA or NDBC was unavailable. The integrated briefing is the source of truth for the combined coastal rating: its production snapshot had four of five inputs available. In the 2026-10-14 16:17 UTC production snapshot, the latest 44007 row included wind but did not include wave height/period/direction. The same snapshot recorded a 6.2 ft wave maximum at 03:50 UTC; that older measurement is useful historical context but cannot be represented as current. The integration was functioning and correctly withheld a current wave value.
